# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 24 October 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and services for downloading and sharing video footage, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- **Username** - chosen by you during registration
- **Email address** - required for new accounts. Used to confirm registration and to recover your password
- **Password** - stored only as a one-way hash (bcrypt). We cannot read your password
- **Avatar** - an image you upload for your profile (optional)
- **Telegram ID** - only for accounts linked to our Telegram bot before email registration was introduced

### 2.2 Temporary Data
- **Verification codes** - a 6-digit code sent to your email for registration or password recovery. It is stored in your browser session only as a hash and expires after 10 minutes
- **Session data** - a session cookie that keeps you logged in

### 2.3 Activity Data
- **Download history** - which footages you download, used for recommendations
- **Category preferences** - the types of content you download most often
- **Activity status** - whether you choose to show or hide your online activity
- **Likes** - which footages you like (shown publicly if you allow it)
- **Comments and chat messages** - text you post under news, collections and in rooms
- **Mentions and notifications** - records of @mentions you receive in rooms, shown to you in the Mentions section

### 2.4 Game Data
- Game state in rooms (Tic-tac-toe and "Guess the word"), including who invited whom and the moves made. Games also store permissions you grant other users to invite you

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To create and secure your account and to let you log in
- To send you codes for registration and password recovery
- To provide and improve our services
- To create recommendations based on your download history
- To display your activity (likes, comments, game results) according to your privacy settings
- To moderate content and prevent abuse

### 3.1 Recommendations
We analyze the types of content you download to suggest similar footages. This analysis uses only your download history and content categories.

### 3.2 Activity Visibility
You control whether other users can see when you are online.

## 4. Email Delivery

We send emails (verification codes and password-recovery codes) through a third-party email service, **Resend** (resend.com). To deliver the message, the service receives your email address and the message text. Resend acts as a data processor for us and may process this data according to its own privacy policy.

No other email is sent to you except messages related to your account and the security of your account.

## 5. Data Security

- Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text
- Verification codes are stored only as hashes and expire after 10 minutes
- After five incorrect code entries, the code is invalidated
- Codes can be requested at most once per minute per address
- We apply reasonable technical measures to protect against unauthorized access. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection

## 6. Your Rights and Controls

You can at any time:
- **Change your nickname**
- **Change your avatar**
- **Change your password**
- **Recover your password** using the email address linked to your account
- **Toggle activity visibility**
- **Delete your account** - contact support. Your personal data will be deleted within 30 days

## 7. Telegram

Telegram is no longer used for logging in. Accounts linked to our Telegram bot earlier continue to work with a password only. We keep the Telegram ID of those accounts so the link is not lost. You can ask us to unlink it.

## 8. User-Generated Content

### 8.1 Comments, Likes and Messages
- Comments, likes and messages are visible to other users according to your privacy settings
- You are responsible for the content you post
- We may remove content that violates our Terms of Use
- Deleting your account does not remove previously posted comments or likes. They may remain attributed to a deleted user

### 8.2 Content Moderation
- Users can submit footages for moderation review
- Moderators review submissions for copyright and policy violations
- **Moderation data** (submitted footages and their status) is kept to improve content quality and prevent abuse. It is **not shared with third parties**

## 9. Data Retention

- Account data is kept while your account is active
- Deleted accounts: personal information is removed within 30 days
- Comments and likes may remain after account deletion
- Verification codes are deleted after expiry or after use

## 10. Third Parties

### 10.1 Who Receives Data
- **Resend** - receives your email address and the text of the code email. Used only to send codes
- **Hosting provider** - stores the site's data on its servers

### 10.2 What We Do Not Do
- We do not sell your personal information
- We do not share your data with advertisers or marketing companies
- We do not use external analytics services for your personal data
- We do not share your download history outside our platform

### 10.3 Internal Analytics
Download history is analyzed only inside our platform, for your personal recommendations.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. For significant changes we will publish the updated version on our website and show the new date at the top.

## 13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us through:
- The support form on our website
- Telegram bot support
